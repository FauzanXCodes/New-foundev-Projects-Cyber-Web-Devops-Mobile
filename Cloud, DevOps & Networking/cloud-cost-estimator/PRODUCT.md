# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Learners, junior cloud practitioners, and portfolio reviewers who need a
transparent way to model a small AWS workload before discussing a cloud-cost
decision. They use the calculator in a focused planning workflow: select a
representative workload, adjust usage and sample rates, inspect the forecast,
then save, compare, or export the result.

## Product Purpose

Cloud Cost Calculator is a dependency-free AWS FinOps planning workbench for
learning and portfolio use. It deliberately demonstrates estimation mechanics
and cost trade-offs without presenting its curated sample rates as live AWS
pricing. Success means a user can understand the model, identify the main cost
drivers, and take a portable estimate into a discussion without hidden state or
surprising behaviour.

## Brand Personality

Clear, grounded, and capable. The voice should explain a cost decision plainly
and avoid both sales pressure and false certainty.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble an official AWS billing console or imply that the sample rates
are a quote. Avoid novelty dashboards, decorative animation, opaque scoring,
and colour-only warnings. The product must not hide normal browser controls
behind custom interactions.

## Design Principles

1. Put the estimate and the assumptions close together so decisions stay
   auditable.
2. Prefer useful FinOps guidance over visual decoration.
3. Keep every workflow reversible, portable, and resilient to malformed input.
4. Make important state readable without relying on colour, hover, or a mouse.
5. Preserve a calm, compact workspace so configuration remains the centre of
   attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard-only operation, visible focus, text
equivalents for charts and status, light/dark/system themes, and reduced
motion. Maintain legible contrast and preserve reflow at narrow widths and
200% zoom.
